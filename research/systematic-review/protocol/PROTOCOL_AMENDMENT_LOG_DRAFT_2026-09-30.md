- **Decision date:** 2026-09-20 (`SECOND_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_v1.0.md`) and 2026-09-21 (`ADJUDICATION_PROTOCOL_v1.0.md`, `FINAL_DISPOSITION_MANIFEST.md`).
