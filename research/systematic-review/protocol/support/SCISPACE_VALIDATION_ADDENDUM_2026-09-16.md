# SCISPACE VALIDATION ADDENDUM — 2026-09-16

## Status

Independent academic-index validation completed using SciSpace.

This validation supplements but does not replace Scopus/Web of Science.

## Main findings

1. No obvious systematic review was found that duplicates the full proposed analytical unit:
   morphology intervention × retrieval paradigm × corpus-level evidence × complementarity.

2. No obvious direct Level-5 study was returned for:
   multiple morphology conditions × same fixed dense retriever × overlap/unique hits/oracle union/hybrid gain.

3. Additional morphology-aware retrieval studies were identified and should enter screening:
   - Fautsch & Savoy (2009), DOI 10.1002/asi.21093
   - Schultz, Honeck & Hahn (2002), DOI 10.3115/1118149.1118158
   - Hahn, Honeck & Schulz (2003), DOI 10.1109/HICSS.2003.1174249
   - Yeshambel, Mothe & Assabie (2020), DOI 10.5220/0010177301180128
   - Kurimo, Creutz & Turunen (2008), DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-04447-2_131
   - Balakrishnan & Lloyd-Yemoh (2014), DOI 10.7763/LNSE.2014.V2.134
   - Darwish, Ali & Abdelali (2014), morphology-equivalence query expansion
   - MORSE-QE (2026), pending independent bibliographic/full-text verification

4. The taxonomy should retain separate categories for:
   raw, stem, lemma, root, morpheme/subword, prefix/character truncation,
   query expansion, script normalization, dense-side morphology,
   representation hybrid, and retrieval hybrid.
